# AI Tools

This section covers operational tooling that helps prepare, run, verify, or maintain AI-assisted development environments. It is intentionally separate from Prompt Engineering and from agent-skill design.

## What belongs here

- repository setup and bootstrap scripts;
- doctor, verification, and maintenance helpers;
- reusable configuration and template assets;
- [repository-owned AI tooling implementation](./repository-owned-ai-tooling.md) architecture, lifecycle, security, and validation guidance;
- [CodexPro](./codexpro/index.md) setup and operational guidance;
- the Codex AI Tooling installer and repository-local AI tooling;
- operational guidance for installing, updating, repairing, and troubleshooting tooling.

## What does not belong here

Prompt-writing methodology belongs under **Prompt Engineering**. MCP concepts and server usage belong under **MCP**. Skill authoring, reusable agent skills, and agent policies belong under **Agents & Skills**.

This separation keeps operational tooling from becoming a catch-all category as the toolkit grows.
